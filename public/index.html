How to read the 3D graph
  • Each dot is one bar of time. Left to right is time (0 = now), depth is price, and height is the third axis you chose.
  • Dot colour is the colour dimension you chose (see the colour bar). Dot size is liquidity: bigger means more orders waiting in the book.
  • Dot shape shows who pushed: ◆ buyers, ■ sellers, ● balanced. A hollow dot is from before order-book recording began.
  • Stem colour: green means more bids waiting, red means more asks waiting. The floor line is the price path.
  • The violet cloud on the right shows where price has tended to go from here in this sample. Denser means more often. It describes the past, not a prediction.
  • Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, click a dot for an explanation, and use the chips to change the view or hide layers.
